Guild Events

Aye, noble members and guildmates of the House of Card and Coin, here is your Guild Event Schedule for the three glorious days of revelry, riddles, and righteous chaos at the Ren Faire. Each day includes three fitting events steeped in the spirit of trade, trickery, and theatrical bravado, with quest prizes and rank rewards woven throughout.

Location
Renaissance Festival, Sunset Park
2601 E. Sunset Rd.
Las Vegas, NV, 89120
Hours

Friday, October 10: 10 a.m.–10 p.m.

Saturday, October 11: 10 a.m.–10 p.m.

Sunday, October 12: 10 a.m.–5 p.m.

Guild Quest Kickoff

Friday, October 10

🕙 Faire Hours: 10 a.m. – 10 p.m.

1. Guild Quest Kickoff – 11:00 a.m.
Stop by the Guild Tent to draw your first Quest Card! Easy challenges for all ages—deliver a secret message, retrieve a bizarre item, or speak in rhymes to a merchant.
🏅Complete quests to earn rank sigils and small prizes.

2. Trick or Trade – 2:00 p.m.
Bring us any odd item, shiny rock, bad poem, or useless bauble. Guild traders will offer ridiculous deals in return.
💰 Barter only! No real coin needed.

3. Campfire Tales & Card Draw – 7:00 p.m.
Join the guild by firelight for a round of storytelling and fortune card draws. Each card grants a small quest or curious blessing.
🃏 Bonus: A secret “Curse Card” is in the deck…

Saturday, October 11

🕙 Faire Hours: 10 a.m. – 10 p.m.

1. Fool’s Errand Scavenger Hunt – 11:30 a.m.
Pick up your Errand Scroll from the guild tent. Tasks include:

  • Find a noble and get their “blessing.”

  • Mimic a bard

  • Trade a joke for a trinket
    🏅Return your completed list to claim a prize and a rank ribbon.

2. Guild Trials: Rock, Scroll, Sword – 3:00 p.m.
Compete in our silly medieval twist on rock-paper-scissors. The winner of the mini-tournament gets a token of guild honor and a loud public cheer.

3. The Masked Mischief Walk – 6:30 p.m.
Throw on a cloak or mask, stay anonymous, and act suspiciously fancy or foolish. A roaming judge will award the most convincingly mysterious guest with a secret quest.

Sunday, October 12

🕙 Faire Hours: 10 a.m. – 5 p.m.

1. One Last Quest – 11:00 a.m.
Final chance to draw a quest, level up your guild rank, or earn your spot in the House’s inner circle. Limited edition rank tags will be given.

2. Court of Coin (Mini Roleplay Skits) – 1:00 p.m.
Guildies and guests may bring “cases” to the Court:
“He stole my turkey leg,” “She hexed my dice,” etc.
Judged by the “Blind Justice of the House.”
🎭 Fun, fast, and full of dramatic nonsense.

3. 🔥 The Roast of the Town Cryer – 4:00 p.m.
Captured and bound (lightly), our beloved Fool faces comedic judgment from the crowd. Step up and hurl your best jests, barbs, and roasts.
🏆 Funniest roast wins the Jester’s Purse and a high rank in the guild (if they survive).
