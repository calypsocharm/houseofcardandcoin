A Wager Before the Faire

The Cardsharp's Hand

Come by the Tavern of an evening and the House deals you a card. One a night, blind, from your own deck — no card comes twice and none may be traded away.

Five nights, five cards, and that is your hand. Turn up four times and you play the four you got. Cannot wait? A card off the House costs coin, and the coin comes from turning up.

Bring your hand to the faire and show it at our table. The better it stands, the better the House pays — and what the House is giving away stays a secret until you show it. There is something in it for the finest hand of the weekend.
