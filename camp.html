Ren Faire Camp

Our Camp at Sunset Park

We claim the camp spot Thursday, October 8 — but guildies won't camp until Friday, October 9. A dry camp under the stars of the Renaissance.

RV Camping

A Weekend Oasis Under the Stars

RV camping (Fifth Wheel, Bumper Pull, or Pop-Up Trailers) lets you park your rig beside the campground and build your own weekend oasis. RV camping days are Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.

The House holds one RV space, and our rig is in it. There is no second spot beside us — if you want to bring your own RV you would need to reserve a space directly with the festival, which runs about $980. Otherwise: pitch a tent, or claim a bunk with us. More in the FAQ.

  • Four-night stay — your own RV in a designated space beside the campground.
  • Six 3-day passes — plus six festival T-shirt vouchers and six festival poster vouchers.
  • Earliest access — we claim the spot Thursday, October 8; guildies camp from Friday, October 9.
  • Private campground — designated potties and private entertainment.

⚠ Dry camping space — no water service, dump station, or servicing is provided by Clark County (contact Las Vegas Toilet Rentals to arrange). RV trailers must unhook from the tow vehicle; no vehicles may remain hitched during the event. Camp spots are very limited and first-come, first-served. No showers on site.

🪓 Facilities & Bathrooms

No showers on site. We have no water service or dump service — shower at home before you come. (There were public showers in the faire grounds last year; that may be an option.) Bathrooms: a potty in the RV, plus porta-potties close by in our camp area. You're welcome to use the refrigerator and sleep in the designated area.

☀️ Heat & Shade — Read This One

Our spot is pavement, with a large generator between the campsites. The year it bit us the air said 97°F — out on the asphalt it felt like 105. There is no shade of its own out there, and blacktop holds the heat long after the sun has come off it.

Do not let a mild forecast fool you. Over the last twenty-six years that weekend has run anywhere from 78°F to 97°F, and last year it rained on the Friday. Come ready for both.

Bring your own shade. A pop-up canopy, a tarp and a bit of rope, anything that gets you out of the sun. It is the single thing that makes the difference between a good day at camp and a rough one.

Bring more water than you think. Not just to drink — for rinsing off, for the back of your neck. There is no water service at camp. A cooler with ice earns its place.

Charging is covered. Plug your phone in inside the RV. There is a large generator sitting between the campsites and it runs the rig, so nobody needs to ration their battery.

Full garb in the afternoon is a lot. Linen and light layers, a hat, and take the middle of the day slowly.

🛏️ Claiming a Bunk?

Bunk 3 has a strong chance of being a cot rather than a bed in the rig — worth knowing before you claim it. Bring your own bedding either way; none is provided. A sleeping bag and your pillow are the way (and yes, maybe a teddy bear). Bunks are limited — release yours if your plans change so a guildmate can take it.

🍗 Food & Drink

We'll have some food at camp and a few beers in the refrigerator, plus a potluck — feel free to eat as you will, in the faire or by the fire. Bring a dish to share if you can.

🪙 Passes

Passes for only six people — and you know who you are. Camp spots are very limited, first-come, first-served.

🎟 Wristbands & the RV Gate

Every bunk comes with a wristband, and that band includes your entry to the faire from the camping area. Show it at the RV campsite gate and the guards will wave you through. No wristband, no entry past the gate.

Do not buy a ticket if you have a bunk. The band already covers your day — there is no need to pay twice.

Your band is yours alone. The faire issues one per camper against our campground code, and every camping attendee needs their own festival pass. Bands are not passed along and camp spots are not transferable — whoever is wearing it is who it was issued to.

📍 Where We Camp

Find the RV Campsites

Look for the camping entrance on the far left of the map — marked with the X. Our RV campsite is just inside. Sunset Park, 2601 E Sunset Rd, Las Vegas.

🚗 Where To Park

Every Lot in the Park

Sunset Park is big, and the faire only uses part of it. Here is the whole park with every public lot marked, so you can see what you are driving into.

If you are camping with us

The faire’s camping FAQ is clear on this much:

  • Park in the regular lots, first come first served. Any pass that comes with the camp reservation is one spot at most, so plan on general parking and a walk in — it is what everyone does.
  • Campers have their own entrance, but you need the credentials to use it.
  • Wristbands come from Mama Bear. The faire sends one code per campground and she collects the bands against it — nothing for you to arrange, just find her at the rig.
  • Every camper still needs their own festival pass. No outside guests on the campsite.
  • No dedicated oversized parking. RVs have preassigned spots; if you are only unloading, there is room for that.

Come early on the Friday if you can — the lots nearest the camping end of the park are the small ones, and they go first.

What is not decided yet

The faire has not published its 2026 parking — their Parking & Rideshare page still says “Information Coming Soon”, and the arrangement changed last year, so nothing from previous years can be trusted. That means general parking, overflow lots and shuttles are all unknown for now.

Read it from them directly: Parking & Rideshare and the Camping FAQs. This page will be updated as soon as they say more.

⚜ The Faire’s Own Rules ⚜

Gates, Tickets & What They Will Not Let In

The camp is ours to run. The gates are not — these are the festival’s, and they are the ones that decide whether you get through the door.

⏰ Gates

  • Friday, Oct 9 — 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.
  • Saturday, Oct 10 — 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.
  • Sunday, Oct 11 — 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.

Sunday finishes five hours earlier than the other two. Worth knowing before you plan the last day.

🎫 Box Office

  • Friday — 9 a.m. to 9 p.m.
  • Saturday — 9 a.m. to 9 p.m.
  • Sunday — 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.

It opens an hour before the gates and shuts an hour before them.

🚪 Getting In

  • No cash at the door. Credit cards only.
  • Buying on the day costs $5 more per ticket than buying online beforehand.
  • Everyone is searched by security on the way in.
  • Have your ticket ready to scan before you reach the front.

If you have a bunk with the House you have a wristband, and the wristband is your way in from the camping area. None of the ticket business above applies to you — do not go and buy one.

🚫 No Animals

The festival allows no pets, no animals and no emotional support animals anywhere on the grounds. Any animal that is not a service animal will be turned away at the gate.

Only service animals as defined by the Americans with Disabilities Act are admitted. They must stay under their handler’s control at all times, and the festival may ask the questions the law allows in order to verify.

This one is theirs, not ours, and they do enforce it — please do not arrive with a dog expecting to talk your way in.
